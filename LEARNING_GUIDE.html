Learning modules &
assessment selection

Three source-aligned learning paths. A traceable content map. A configurable word-and-phrase practice builder.

What changed

Choose course → module → category → words / phrases → exact count → assessment type.

The new Learning section contains short explanations, model exchanges, individual word/phrase cards, optional audio, Thai meaning support, and clearly identified teacher tasks. Learning checkmarks are self-study records, separate from assessment accuracy.

Seven assessment choices are implemented: multiple choice, matching pairs, type the answer, word in context, sentence builder, listen & select, and mixed practice. Presets are 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 and 50, with a custom number up to the available pool. Manual target selection and missed-item-only practice are supported. Entries are never repeated to fill a requested count.

Practice gives immediate feedback. Self-check mode defers feedback until the end. Matching uses immediate feedback and records first-attempt accuracy. Listening with a revealed transcript is explicitly marked text-supported, not unassisted listening. Typed answers recall the course form; punctuation and capitalisation are ignored, but free translations are not automatically judged.

Source conflicts — confirmation needed

Course durations conflict

The presentation summary (p.2) says 40 hours for each specialist course, while its detailed tables (pp.6–7) say 20. The Spa DOCX states 20 hours in the structure and 15 in DURATION. The Hair & Nail DOCX says TOTAL: 15 HOURS and totals 9 theory + 11 practice, but the listed day rows sum to 7.5 theory + 10.5 practice = 18. Do not publish an approved specialist duration until NITS confirms it.

Foundation hour total and numbering

The A1 rows in the ECSE DOCX/presentation sum to 46 hours, not the stated 40. With the 20-hour interview module, the rows total 66, not 60. The numbering jumps from Module 6 to Module 8. Original numbers are retained; no Module 7 is invented.

Safety outcome without a numbered module

ECSE includes safety/emergency communication in its objectives and ELO5, but no corresponding numbered module appears in its structure. Existing safety content is displayed as a supplementary learning section, not a silently added official Module 7.

Hair & Nail slide contains spa-specific wording

Presentation p.7 still describes a Spa Therapist and spa/massage experience in Days 1–2. The Hair & Nail DOCX supplies the role-specific Day 1/2 framing used by this app. Both source versions remain noted; this is an editorial implementation choice, not a source correction.

Practice is not course certification or placement

Source assessment plans include CV, speaking, role-play and mock interview evidence. Auto-marked word/phrase practice does not replace these. The presentation’s placement table also requires interview and listening/speaking evidence; this app does not infer Marlins scores, CEFR level, recruitment eligibility or official course passes.

Remaining content gaps

The sources give syllabus outlines, not full lesson texts, employer briefings or complete CV/technical assessment materials. Short lessons, career/hair/nail entries and model exchanges in this release are newly authored support. Employer/product facts, occupational technique, full CV marking and spoken interview judgements still need institution-approved materials and a qualified teacher.

Verification

160 structural/selection checks and 75 browser checks passed. The browser suite completed all seven new assessment types across three courses, a delayed-feedback check per course, manual selection, missed-only retry, lower later scores, course separation, Thai UI switching, and responsive layouts at 320, 390 and 1280 pixels. No JavaScript page errors were captured in that suite.

The browser tests used actual app scripts/styles in an in-memory Chromium page. Repetitive answer loops used DOM click events; form fill/check/select operations used browser controls. Local storage, speech and selected transport calls were mocked. Direct HTTP/file navigation was blocked by the authoring environment. Live PHP/MySQL persistence, production deployment, audible voice quality, microphones, Safari/iOS, independent translation accuracy and psychometric validity were not verified.

The test scripts and machine-readable evidence are included under tests/learning-* and tests/validate_learning.cjs. Original test artifacts are historical evidence, not results of this release.

Open and deploy

For a quick look, open the standalone preview and choose Continue as guest. It contains the frontend and content, but no account server. Guest work is session-only.

For the full application, extract the ZIP and serve nimmanspa/index.html. Keep the provided folder structure. Account use requires the existing PHP/MySQL setup in backend/README.md. The original spa and cruise course IDs are unchanged; the new course ID is salon. No schema change was needed for the existing JSON progress endpoint. Back up production files and the database before replacing files, retain the existing private backend configuration, and verify on staging first.

The account save guard prevents a failed initial load from submitting a blank replacement record. It is not a complete offline synchronisation or multi-device conflict-resolution system. After a load error, restore the connection and reopen the course. Current temporary edits made while loading failed are not merged automatically.

Detailed attempt answers can be exported during the result session. The account record stores the latest 50 compact attempt summaries and per-entry review status; this limits payload size. The original activity library remains separate from module-filtered custom practice.
