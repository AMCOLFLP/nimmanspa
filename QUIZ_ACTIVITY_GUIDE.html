Run a useful practice session

Choose the level and length

Open Practice. Choose 5, 10, 15 or 20 items and Starter, Everyday, Extended or All levels. Difficulty labels are editorial, not certified CEFR levels. Unseen questions are prioritised; then older attempts return.

Readings keep all three questions for a passage together: the 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 settings normally yield 3 / 9 / 15 / 18 questions when enough passages match. Dialogues use their own number of blanks. Small filtered banks return fewer items.

Understand feedback, then retry

Learners receive a model answer and an explanation. Newly authored items include Thai support. The results page retains their first response and offers Retry missed items and New practice set.

Retrying does not overwrite the original activity score. Review my mistakes draws from missed questions in the current course across levels. A correct answer with listening support does not clear a previous mistake.

Teach complete communication

Use workplace readings to find the purpose, important details and next action. Use Listen & respond to identify the guest’s request before choosing a reply. Follow with role-play in pairs.

Audio uses browser speech synthesis, not human recordings. Opening a transcript or answering without requesting playback marks supported practice. The app cannot verify that the learner actually heard the sound.

Build, order and retrieve

Tap words or conversation turns in order. Tap a placed item to remove it, or use Clear. Typed context answers ignore case and surrounding punctuation and accept only listed variants, not approximate guesses.

Use the first-letter and meaning clues to target a specific word. Avoid interpreting a single correct response as mastery or a formal proficiency result.

Workplace examples are fictional. Confirm actual prices, times, locations, permission, compensation and safety arrangements with the workplace. The language examples are not treatment protocols, medical advice, emergency training or independent operational authorisation.

Search the question and answer bank

Choose a course, activity or level. Search English or Thai text, a topic or an item ID. Open an answer panel to see the model and feedback. Choices shown here are in editorial order; the learner app shuffles them.

Progress and privacy. Demo account progress remains browser-local and course-specific; guest progress lasts for the session. The inherited authentication is not production-secure. Use test passwords only. No cross-device sync or live deployment was added.
